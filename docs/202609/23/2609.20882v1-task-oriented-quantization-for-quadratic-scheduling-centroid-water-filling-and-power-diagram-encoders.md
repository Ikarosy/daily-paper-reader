## 摘要
我们区分了在已知确定性 oracle 动作下面向任务量化的两种情形。对于无约束的内点 oracle 和光滑强凹效用，通过向量 Lloyd-Max 量化 oracle 动作可最小化均方误差代理，并对最优 K 级任务量化器达到 β/α 近似。该归约对各向同性二次损失是精确的，且相应的任务率-失真函数被两个普通率-失真函数所界定。预算约束的二次调度则不同：oracle 满足一个变分不等式，因此量化注水动作通常并非最优。我们推导了该情形下的精确 Lloyd 型条件。量化器单元的最优动作是在该单元的条件均值负载处评估的注水，而最优编码器将负载空间划分为仿射幂图单元。因此正确的方案是量化负载并对其质心进行注水。当一个单元跨越注水有效集边界时，这一区别至关重要。

## Abstract
We distinguish two regimes in task-oriented quantization with a known deterministic oracle action. For an unconstrained interior oracle and a smooth strongly concave utility, quantizing the oracle action by vector Lloyd-Max minimizes a mean-squared-error surrogate and achieves a $β/α$ approximation to the optimal $K$-level task quantizer. The reduction is exact for isotropic quadratic loss, and the corresponding task rate--distortion function is bracketed by two ordinary rate--distortion functions. Budget-constrained quadratic scheduling is different: the oracle satisfies a variational inequality, so quantizing water-filled actions is not generally optimal. We derive the exact Lloyd-type conditions for this case. The optimal action for a quantizer cell is water-filling evaluated at the cell's conditional-mean load, and the optimal encoder partitions load space into affine power-diagram cells. Thus the correct prescription is to quantize the load and water-fill its centroid. The distinction is material whenever a cell crosses water-filling active-set boundaries.
